Why Fueling Shifts Between Seasons Endurance athletes know that training doesn’t stop when the weather changes, it just means more training will take place indoors. But this shift also means the body’s fueling and hydration needs change dramatically. Environmental factors, session structure, and physiological stress all change how your body uses energy and fluids.
